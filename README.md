## Problem

Organisations making million-pound decisions 
about transport infrastructure, media investment, 
and urban planning are working with static 
reports and gut instinct.

BT's mobile network data captures anonymised 
movement patterns across the UK — where people 
go, when, how long they stay, and how they move 
between locations.

The gap: this data exists but it takes weeks 
of analyst time to turn it into actionable 
intelligence for decision makers.

## The Opportunity

AI can transform raw mobile network data into 
instant, sector-specific intelligence reports 
in seconds — making BT's data assets accessible 
to decision makers who are not data scientists.

## Two Verticals. One Engine.

### 🚌 Transport Intelligence
For: Transport authorities, government 
departments, infrastructure consultancies

Outputs:
- Origin-destination flow analysis
- Traffic impact assessments
- Event impact modelling
- Real-time congestion intelligence
- Infrastructure investment recommendations

### 📺 Media Intelligence  
For: Media planners, advertising agencies,
brand managers

Outputs:
- OOH location recommendations by audience
- Peak footfall times for target segments
- Campaign reach and attribution estimates
- Audience profile from movement data
- Media spend justification narrative

## Who It's For

**Transport:** Highway engineers, transport 
planners, local authority decision makers, 
infrastructure consultancies

**Media:** Media planners at agencies, 
brand managers, DOOH buyers, 
BT Active Intelligence sales team

## Core Product Decisions

1. **Sector selector at entry point** — same 
   underlying data engine, sector-specific 
   output format. One product, two markets.

2. **Plain English input** — users are not 
   data scientists. Campaign briefs and 
   transport questions in natural language, 
   structured intelligence output.

3. **Confidence scoring mandatory** — 
   recommendations backed by data volume 
   indicators. Low data density areas 
   flagged explicitly.

4. **Narrative output not just numbers** — 
   decision makers need to justify spend 
   to boards and councils. AI generates 
   the rationale, not just the data.
